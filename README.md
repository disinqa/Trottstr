# trottstr

![trottstr](assets/images/trottstr.png)

Globetrotter's travel log for Android. Tracks your days of stay in each jurisdiction to easily navigate tax residency, visa and citizenship requirements. Powered by Nostr and vibed with Purplestack.

## Features

- Track your travel history and days spent in each jurisdiction
- View your travel history on a map
- Export your travel history as a CSV file
